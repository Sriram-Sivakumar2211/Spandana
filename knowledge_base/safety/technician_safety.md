# Industrial Technician Safety & Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) Protocol

## Mandatory Safety Directives

### 1. Electrical Lockout / Tagout (LOTO)
- **Zero Energy State Verification**: Before opening terminal boxes or performing mechanical bearing servicing, disconnect main circuit breaker, apply lockout padlock, attach tag with technician contact, and test for voltage zero at motor terminals using a calibrated Multimeter.
- **VFD Capacitor Discharge**: Variable Frequency Drives store hazardous DC bus voltage in capacitors for up to 10 minutes after power cutoff. Wait minimum 10 minutes and measure DC bus voltage ($<50\text{V}$) before servicing VFD-connected motors.

### 2. Thermal & Surface Hazards
- Motor frame and bearing housings can reach temperatures exceeding 100°C (212°F). Allow motor 30 minutes cooling time or wear thermal gloves rated for minimum 250°C before touching casing.

### 3. Rotating Equipment Safety
- Never wear loose clothing, jewelry, or neckties near rotating shafts, fan cowls, or flexible couplings.
- Ensure coupling guards and fan cowls are fully secured in place before energizing the motor.

### 4. Chemical Safety (Solvents & Synthetic Greases)
- Wear nitrile gloves when handling synthetic lubricants or cleaning solvents.
- Consult Safety Data Sheets (SDS) for chemical exposure limits and emergency eyewash procedures.
