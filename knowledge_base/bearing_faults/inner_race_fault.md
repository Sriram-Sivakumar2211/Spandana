# Inner Race Defect & Maintenance Standard Operating Procedure

## Overview & Fault Description
An inner race bearing fault occurs when micro-spalling, fatigue cracking, or pitting develops along the rotating inner raceway of a rolling element bearing. Because the inner race rotates with the shaft under cyclic load, impact energy generates transient pulses at the Ball Pass Frequency Inner Race (BPFI).

## Diagnostic Evidence & Sensor Signatures
- **Vibration Harmonics**: High frequency impacts at the BPFI frequency (typically 4x to 8x shaft rotational speed). Amplitude modulation of BPFI by shaft rotational frequency (1X sidebands).
- **Kurtosis & Crest Factor**: Elevated kurtosis (>4.5) and crest factor during early micro-spalling stages due to sharp impact transients.
- **RMS Acceleration**: Progressive increase in RMS vibration acceleration as raceway degradation advances.
- **Acoustic / Thermal**: Audible clicking or high-pitched whining; localized thermal rise at shaft bearing journal.

## Root Causes
1. **Shaft Fit Inaccuracies**: Excessive interference fit causing inner ring hoop stress or tight internal radial clearance.
2. **Contamination**: Hard particle ingress creating stress risers on the raceway surface.
3. **Inadequate Lubrication**: Breakdown of elastohydrodynamic (EHD) oil film causing metal-to-metal contact.
4. **Shaft Currents**: Electrical fluting or frosting caused by VFD-induced shaft voltage discharging across the inner raceway.

## Inspection & Remediation Protocol
1. **Visual & Dimensional Inspection**:
   - Disassemble housing and inspect inner raceway under magnifying loupe or borescope for circumferential spalling.
   - Check shaft journal tolerances using a micrometer to verify fit spec.
2. **Lubrication Maintenance**:
   - Purge old grease and inspect for metallic debris using a magnet test.
   - Replenish with ISO VG 100 or NLGI Grade 2 synthetic grease containing anti-wear (EP) additives.
3. **Corrective Action**:
   - If spalling depth exceeds 0.5 mm or BPFI amplitude continues to rise, schedule bearing replacement during the next maintenance window.
   - Install shaft grounding rings if electrical fluting is observed.
