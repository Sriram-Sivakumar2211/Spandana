# Outer Race Defect & Maintenance Standard Operating Procedure

## Overview & Fault Description
An outer race fault occurs when fatigue spalling, fluting, or corrosion defects form on the stationary outer raceway of the bearing. Since the outer ring is generally stationary within the housing, impacts occur as rolling elements cross the stationary load zone at the Ball Pass Frequency Outer Race (BPFO).

## Diagnostic Evidence & Sensor Signatures
- **Vibration Harmonics**: Distinct impacts at the BPFO frequency (typically 2.5x to 4.5x shaft speed). Unlike inner race faults, BPFO harmonics usually lack 1X shaft sidebands because the load zone position remains fixed relative to the defect.
- **Spectrum & Envelope Analysis**: Dominant peak at BPFO in envelope spectrum with multiple harmonics (2xBPFO, 3xBPFO).
- **Spectral Energy**: Energy concentration in 2 kHz – 10 kHz frequency bands.
- **Housing Temperature**: Elevated bearing housing temperature exceeding 70°C (158°F).

## Root Causes
1. **Housing Misalignment**: Distortion of outer ring due to out-of-round housing bore or soft foot condition.
2. **Fretting Corrosion**: Micro-motion between outer ring external surface and housing bore due to loose fit.
3. **Overloading**: Static overload or heavy axial thrust force distorting the raceway geometry.
4. **Moisture Contamination**: Water entry leading to localized raceway oxidation and pitting.

## Inspection & Remediation Protocol
1. **Housing & Alignment Check**:
   - Inspect housing bore for fretting corrosion, wear marks, or out-of-round distortion.
   - Verify mounting bolts torque and check for soft foot using a dial indicator.
2. **Vibration Trend Monitoring**:
   - Track BPFO peak amplitude over 24–48 hours. Rapid doubling of BPFO indicates imminent severe spalling.
3. **Corrective Action**:
   - Re-align housing and adjust bearing axial clearance.
   - Clean housing bore, apply anti-fretting compound (Molykote), or replace housing if worn beyond tolerance.
   - Replace bearing assembly if defect severity is moderate to critical.
