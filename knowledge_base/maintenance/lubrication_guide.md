# Industrial Lubrication & Bearing Maintenance Guide

## Overview & Importance
Proper lubrication reduces friction, dissipates thermal energy, prevents corrosion, and seals bearings against abrasive particulate ingress. Over 40% of premature bearing failures in MSME manufacturing plants are caused by lubrication issues (under-greasing, over-greasing, or lubricant contamination).

## Lubricant Selection Matrix
- **Grease Selection**: NLGI Grade 2 lithium complex or polyurea grease with ISO VG 100-220 base oil for standard electric motors (1500–3600 RPM).
- **High-Temperature Duty**: Polyurea grease with synthetic polyalphaolefin (PAO) base oil for motor operating temperatures >90°C.
- **Extreme Pressure (EP)**: EP additives recommended for heavy radial load or shock load applications.

## Regreasing Quantity & Interval Calculation
- **Grease Quantity ($G$)**:
  $$G = 0.005 \times D \times B \quad [\text{grams}]$$
  where $D$ = Bearing Outer Diameter (mm), $B$ = Bearing Width (mm).
- **Regreasing Interval**:
  For standard 4-pole motor operating 16 hours/day: Regrease every 2,000 to 3,000 operating hours or 6 months.

## Best Practices & Hazards
1. **Clean Fitting First**: Wipe Zerk grease fitting with clean lint-free cloth before attaching grease gun to prevent forcing external dirt into the bearing cavity.
2. **Open Drain Plug**: Remove bottom grease drain plug during regreasing; allow motor to run 15–30 minutes to purge excess grease before re-installing plug.
3. **Avoid Over-Greasing**: Excess grease causes hydrodynamic churning, leading to rapid temperature spikes (>100°C) and grease thermal separation.
