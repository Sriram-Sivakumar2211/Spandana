# Abnormal Overall Vibration & Structural Looseness

## Overview & Fault Description
Abnormal overall vibration refers to un-isolated mechanical oscillations resulting from structural looseness, resonance, foundation distortion, or worn machine mounts. Looseness alters system stiffness dynamically during each shaft revolution.

## Diagnostic Evidence & Sensor Signatures
- **Multi-Harmonic Peaks**: Rich spectrum of multiple running speed harmonics (1X, 2X, 3X, 4X, ..., 10X) and fractional sub-harmonics (0.5X).
- **Directional Variation**: Radial vibration in horizontal direction is often 3x to 5x greater than vertical due to foundation compliance.
- **RMS Spike**: Sharp increase in overall velocity RMS across 10 Hz – 1000 Hz.

## Root Causes
1. **Structural Looseness (Type A)**: Loose foot bolts, cracked baseplate welds, or degraded grout.
2. **Internal Looseness (Type B)**: Worn bearing sleeve, loose bearing fit in end-bell housing.
3. **Resonance**: Operating rotational speed or component defect frequency matching a structural natural frequency.

## Remediation Protocol
1. **Bump Test / Natural Frequency Check**: Perform impact test with soft hammer to identify structural natural frequencies.
2. **Torque Check**: Re-torque foundation bolts and motor hold-down bolts to spec using calibrated torque wrench.
3. **Grout & Baseplate Repair**: Repair cracked concrete grout pads using high-strength epoxy grout.
